Read the full privacy policy.

  • Transcription, speaker recognition, translation and summaries run on your computer.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no subscription and no ads.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, once the models are downloaded. Each model is downloaded only once, the first time you use it.

Store or installer: which one should I pick?

The Microsoft Store version installs in one click and is updated by the Store. The installer from GitHub updates itself from inside the app. They are the same app.

How good are the summaries?

They are made by a small AI model on your PC: good at the main points, but they can get a detail wrong. Check important details in the transcript.

I had MediaScribe. Is this the same app?

Yes. MediaScribe is now Homeword: same app, new name. MediaScribe updates itself to Homeword and keeps your settings, recent jobs and downloaded models.
